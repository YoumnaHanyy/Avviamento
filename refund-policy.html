AVEST. IN STYLE

01 / YOUR OPTIONS

The right piece matters.

Timeframes start on the day you receive your order. The rights below are subject to the applicable exceptions under Egyptian consumer protection rules.

01 / CHANGE OF MIND↗

Return or exchange

14 days from receipt

You can request a return or exchange without giving a reason, where the item is eligible under Egyptian consumer protection rules.

02 / SOMETHING WRONG↗

Faulty item

30 days from receipt

If an item has a defect, you can request an exchange or return. Contact us with your order number and a description of the issue.

For a qualifying defect, the exchange or return is handled without an additional cost to you. Your statutory rights continue to apply. Read the Egyptian Consumer Protection Agency guidance.

02 / HOW IT WORKS

Simple from the start.

01

Get in touch

Use our Contact Us page and share your order number, the item and whether you would prefer a return or exchange. If something is damaged or faulty, describe the issue and include photos if available.

02

Keep the item ready

For a change of mind, please keep the item in the condition in which you received it. Keep its labels and packaging where possible while we review your request.

03

Arrange the next step

Our team will explain how to return the item or arrange an exchange. Any applicable delivery details will be confirmed with you before collection or shipping.
